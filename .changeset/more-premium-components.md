Add 12 more customizable components:

- **Charts:** `ScatterChart` (bubble sizes, trendlines), `RadarChart`, `WaterfallChart`, `CandlestickChart` (OHLC/candle/line, volume, SMA/EMA overlays, crosshair, pan/zoom), `SankeyChart`, `CalendarHeatmap`, and `GeoMap` (choropleth + bubble markers over caller-supplied paths, with a `geoJsonToRegions` helper).
- **Layout / data:** `OrgChart` (collapsible, vertical or horizontal, custom node cards) and `DashboardGrid` (draggable, resizable tile layout with collision pushing and keyboard editing).
- **UX:** `FilterBar` (typed conditions, saved views, `applyFilters`), `ProductTour` (spotlight onboarding), `VirtualList` (fixed, known or measured row heights; infinite scroll), `TransferList` and `TagInput`.
